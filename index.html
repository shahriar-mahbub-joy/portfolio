Statement of Interest

I'm a final-year LLB student at BRAC University, drawn to the parts of law that happen outside the courtroom — legal aid clinics, community outreach, and the slow, unglamorous work of documentation that makes advocacy possible. My CGPA sits at 3.43, but the more telling record is what I've chosen to spend my time on: running a youth welfare organisation, leading the university's law society, and interning with a humanitarian law NGO.

I'm especially interested in legal aid, human rights, and public interest law — work where research has to translate into something a person without legal training can actually use. That's the throughline across everything below: programme coordination, advocacy, and writing that's meant to be read, not just filed.
